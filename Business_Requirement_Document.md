# Business Requirement Document (BRD)
## Project: Telco Customer Churn & Revenue Protection Analysis

### 1. Executive Summary
The objective of this project is to analyze customer attrition (churn) patterns for a telecommunications provider. By identifying key behavioral, demographic, and financial drivers of churn, this project aims to provide actionable strategy recommendations to improve customer retention and protect Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

---

### 2. Business Problem & Objectives
* **Core Problem:** The business is experiencing customer loss, directly impacting long-term profitability and increasing Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC).
* **Primary Objective:** Quantify total churn, evaluate MRR impact, and isolate high-risk customer segments.
* **Secondary Objective:** Formulate data-driven retention strategies (contract incentives, service add-on optimizations, billing friction reduction).

---

### 3. Key Analytical Questions & Hypotheses
1. **Contract Structure:** Do month-to-month contracts exhibit significantly higher churn rates compared to long-term contracts?
2. **Payment Methods:** Does billing friction in specific payment methods (e.g., electronic checks) correlate with churn?
3. **Service Attach Rates:** Does subscribing to technical support or security add-ons increase customer retention?
4. **Tenure Dynamics:** At what month threshold does customer churn peak?

---

### 4. Target Metrics & KPIs
* **Churn Rate (%):** Percentage of total customers lost.
* **Total Lost MRR ($):** Total monthly recurring revenue lost to churned accounts.
* **Average Tenure (Months):** Average duration a churned customer stays before canceling.
* **High-Risk Segment Share (%):** Proportion of churn concentrated in specific contract/payment buckets.
